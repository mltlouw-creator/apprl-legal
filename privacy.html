Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 May 2026

12 Tables Holdings LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Apprl mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Apprl:

  • First name — entered by you during onboarding
  • Device identifier — a unique ID generated on your device to associate your data with your account
  • Wardrobe data — clothing photos, item descriptions, categories, colours, and other attributes you add
  • Outfit and wear history — outfit suggestions you generate and items you mark as worn
  • Referral information — if you were referred by another user or share a referral link
  • Push notification token — to send you outfit reminders and app updates (only if you grant permission)
  • Subscription status — your current plan tier, managed via Apple App Store and RevenueCat

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App's core features (wardrobe management, AI outfit suggestions)
  • To process and display your clothing data and outfit history
  • To send push notifications (only with your permission)
  • To manage your subscription and process payments via Apple App Store
  • To send onboarding and re-engagement emails (only if you provide an email address)
  • To operate our referral programme

3. AI Processing

When you upload clothing photos for AI analysis, those images are sent to OpenAI's API for processing. OpenAI processes the image to identify clothing attributes (category, colour, style, etc.) and returns a description. We do not store images with OpenAI beyond the duration of the API call. OpenAI's privacy policy applies to this processing: openai.com/privacy.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the App:

  • Supabase — database and file storage (EU servers)
  • OpenAI — AI-powered clothing analysis
  • OneSignal — push notifications
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Brevo — transactional email delivery
  • Apple App Store — payment processing

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and data practices. We encourage you to review them.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase servers located in the European Union (eu-west-2 region). We implement industry-standard security measures including row-level security on our database. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as you use the App. If you wish to have your data deleted, contact us at mltlouw@gmail.com and we will remove your data within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict our processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at mltlouw@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or email. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
